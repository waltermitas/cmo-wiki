## 1. THE STORY IN ONE PARAGRAPH

FC Barcelona is launching **Barça Banc** — the club's official Visa card and rewards programme — in Latin America ahead of the 2026 FIFA World Cup. The card gives the club's 428 million fans what decades of supporting FC Barcelona never did: a financial product that pays them back for every match, every purchase, every moment of loyalty. Barça Banc goes live in Mexico during the World Cup opening match on June 11, followed by Brazil and Argentina on June 12–13, and Colombia on June 18–22. It is not a crypto product. It is a Visa card, backed by one of the most recognized brands on earth, timed to the most-watched sporting event in history.

---

## 2. BACKGROUND

### What Barça Banc Is

Barça Banc is FC Barcelona's official financial product: a Visa card with an FCB Rewards Points programme built specifically for the club's fan base. Cardholders earn FCB Points on every purchase, with multipliers during matches, win bonuses when their country or FCB wins, and Player-of-the-Match cashback when a selected Barça player earns the award.

Points redeem against real FCB experiences — Camp Nou seats, hospitality, signed merchandise, and exclusive access. This is not a loyalty points programme that eventually converts to a discount at an unrelated retailer. The rewards are the club.

### The Partnership

Barça Banc is the result of a partnership between **FC Barcelona** and **LFG Holdings**, a fintech investment group. LFG provides the financial infrastructure; FC Barcelona provides the brand, the player relationships, and the distribution reach.

### Why LatAm, Why the World Cup, Why Now

Three things converge in June 2026:

1. **The World Cup is in North America** — Mexico, the USA, and Canada host. Brazil, Mexico, and Argentina are all in the tournament, and all three are Phase 1 Barça Banc markets.
2. **FCB's player roster spans the entire LatAm fanbase** — Raphinha anchors Brazil, the full Spanish squad (Pedri, Lamine Yamal, Gavi, Fermín) anchors Spanish-language LatAm, and FCB alumni provide cultural reach in Argentina and Mexico.
3. **LatAm neobank adoption is accelerating** — Brazil's Nubank serves 100M+ users. Mexico and Colombia have the fastest-growing mobile banking penetration in the region. The market is ready; the timing is right.

The World Cup window (June 11 – July 19) is the single highest-engagement moment a football brand can occupy. Barça Banc is designed to be live during every Phase 1 market's opening match.

## 4. THE LOYALTY DIVIDEND

The launch's headline mechanic is the **Loyalty Dividend** — a personalized card generated for every fan showing the FCB Points they would have earned if Barça Banc had existed when they became a culer.

> *"12 years a culer. €4,200 of love. Earn it back, every match."*

Every fan who signs up for the waitlist receives their personalized Loyalty Dividend card, calculated against their FCB account history. The card is built to share — one tap to WhatsApp or Instagram Stories — with a pre-written caption embedding their personal number.

**Why this is news:** It converts 428 million people's football fandom into a specific, personalized financial figure for the first time. Journalists can generate their own card. Their readers can generate their own card.

**Precedent:** The mechanic is modeled on Surgence Labs' prior work — the ETHGas Gas Report (590,000 user shares) and the ORBT DeFi Score (70,000 shares). The Loyalty Dividend is the same mechanic, applied to the largest fanbase in sport.

## 5. THE WORLD CUP MECHANICS

Once the card is live, six match-day mechanics activate across all Phase 1 markets:

| Mechanic | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Country Boost Days** | 3–5× FCB Points during your country's match window |
| **Win Bonus** | +5% points retroactively credited when your country wins |
| **Player-of-the-Match Cashback** | +10% FCB Points when your selected Barça player is named MOTM |
| **Country-Edition Cards** | 5,000 limited-run cards per country in national colors — pre-order opens May 24 |
| **Bracket Prediction Game** | In-app bracket, free entry, daily DAU driver for the full 39-day tournament |
| **Final Ticket VIP Draw** | 50 seats to the World Cup Final (MetLife Stadium, July 19), flights and hotel included; entries earned by card spend, match wins, and waitlist tier |

The compound effect: a Brazilian fan supporting both Barça and Brazil earns multiplied points every time Raphinha plays — whether for FCB or for the Seleção.

## 6. SUGGESTED STORY ANGLES

The following angles are offered as editorial starting points. The PR team should adapt them for each publication's audience and voice.

### Angle 1: The Biggest Brand in Football Just Became Your Bank
**Target:** Financial and general news (FT, Bloomberg, Reuters, Forbes)
FC Barcelona — 428 million fans, 167 million Instagram followers, the most-followed sports club on earth — is entering financial services. Not via an app partnership or co-branded card with an anonymous fintech. Via its own product, its own brand, its own rewards. The question is: what happens when sport becomes a bank?

### Angle 2: The World Cup Is the Launch Event
**Target:** Sports business media (The Athletic, ESPN, Sports Illustrated, Marca, Globo Esporte)
Every Barça Banc launch moment is anchored to a World Cup match. Mexico goes live during Mexico's opening match. Brazil during Brazil's opening match. The card earns more when your country scores. It is not a coincidence — the entire go-to-market is designed around 39 days of the highest-attention sports content on the planet.

### Angle 3: The Neobank Battle for LatAm's Football Fans
**Target:** Fintech / LatAm business media (Rest of World, Contxto, Bloomberg Línea, Folha, Infobae)
Nubank built the most valuable neobank in the world on Brazilian football culture. Barça Banc is arriving in the same market, backed by the club that Brazilians watch La Liga for, at the moment of peak football emotion. How do you compete with Nubank in Brazil? You sign Raphinha and go live during the opening match.

### Angle 4: The Loyalty Dividend — What 12 Years of Supporting FC Barcelona Is Actually Worth
**Target:** Feature / culture media (GQ, ESPN Magazine, The Athletic long-form)
Generate your own Loyalty Dividend card. Run the number. Now tell me that's not a story about fandom and money and what loyalty really costs. This is the piece that every sports writer who has ever bought a kit, a ticket, or a streaming subscription has a personal angle into.

### Angle 5: The Country-Edition Cards — Limited, National, Gone
**Target:** Design, culture, and sneakerhead-adjacent media (Hypebeast, Highsnobiety, Dezeen, Copa90)
5,000 cards per country, in national colors, only for verified residents of that country. They ship to the first activators in each market. The design language: FCB visual codes (garnet and blue) blended with the color palette of each nation's football culture — not generic flag colors, the specific visual vocabulary of that country's fanbase. When Brazil wins, a Champion Card ships.

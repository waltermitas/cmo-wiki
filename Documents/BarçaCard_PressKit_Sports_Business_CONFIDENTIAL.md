## 1. THE STORY IN ONE PARAGRAPH

FC Barcelona is launching **Barça Card** — the club's official Visa card and rewards programme — in Latin America ahead of the 2026 FIFA World Cup. The card gives the club's 428 million fans something decades of supporting FC Barcelona never did: a financial product that pays them back for every match, every purchase, every moment of loyalty. Barça Card goes live in Mexico during the World Cup opening match on June 11 at Estadio Azteca, followed by Brazil and Argentina on June 12–13, and Colombia on June 18–22. It is not a crypto product. It is a Visa card — backed by one of the most recognized brands on earth, timed to the most-watched sporting event in history.

## 4. THE ULTIMATE FAN: THE PRE-LAUNCH MECHANIC

The pre-launch campaign centers on a single question every Barça fan has debated for years: **Are you the Ultimate Barça Fan?**

Starting May 24, fans can take a short identity challenge at card.fcbarcelona.com and claim their personal **Ultimate Fan Card** — a shareable digital card that reveals their Barça archetype (one of twelve, from *The Original* to *The Lamine Generation*), their global Ultimate Score, and their rank among all Barça fans worldwide. Claiming the card is the pre-registration.

The mechanic is built to spread the way football conversations actually spread in Latin America: through WhatsApp group chats, Instagram Stories, and the group dynamics of a fanbase that has been arguing about Barça for decades. Fans challenge each other. Country leaderboards run from announcement day — Brazil vs. Mexico vs. Argentina vs. Colombia. The ranked fan with the highest pre-registration score in each country earns priority card access and the Country-Edition Card in their national colors.

There is no traditional waitlist funnel. The card *is* the product. The waitlist is the byproduct.

**The twelve archetypes:**
The Original · Joga Bonito Believer · Tiki-Taka Architect · Pep-Era Survivor · The Witness · MSN Faithful · Galáctico Resister · Legacy Carrier · Built For Away Nights · The Rebuilder · Lamine Generation · The Pilgrim

**Why this is news:** Every Barça fan who reads about the launch can take the quiz and have a personal result in under a minute. The story is not just about the card — it is about which archetype they are. That is shareable content the journalist can generate for themselves and embed in coverage.

## 7. THE SPORTS BUSINESS STORY

Barça Card is a sports business story before it is a fintech story. The framing for business and sports press:

**FC Barcelona earns approximately €0.25 per fan per year** from 428 million global supporters. Nubank, by comparison, earns approximately €80 per user per year from a Brazilian user base. The gap between those two numbers is the entire strategic rationale for Barça Card.

The comparison is direct: Nubank spent years and billions building a brand identity in Brazil that FC Barcelona already has. Chime spent $519.8 million in marketing to reach the kind of cultural penetration that 428 million FCB fans represent by definition. Barça Card starts where those companies spent decades arriving.

This is not a one-off card deal. The World Cup window (June 11 – July 19) is a 39-day acquisition event, and the card infrastructure is built for the long-term: local payment rails in each market, a rewards system tied to the full FC Barcelona content and events calendar, and a player relationship that makes every transfer window a product news cycle.
